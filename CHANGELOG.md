# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-27

First release: 19 skills in six plugins (core, feedback, discovery, planning, experiments, decisions) on
`https://topy.ai/mcp/cpo`, ported from Maggie Product and Maggie Feedback Triage — the CPO's goals and status report for
the CEO, the weekly product review (Tuesday 09:27) and the quiet daily watch (weekdays 11:07), feedback capture with
de-identification, triage with accept/reject/duplicate/needs-info, small/medium/large and agent-vs-human ownership,
closing the loop with the sender, user research, the PMF survey, positioning with a sourced competitor teardown, RICE
prioritisation, the now/next/later roadmap, PRDs with a success metric and how it is measured, build or not before the
AI CTO, pre-registered experiments read without peeking, instrumenting and shipping behind a flag, and a decision log
with falsifiers and revisits. The CPO decides what to build; the AI CTO builds. Packages: `@topy-ai/cpo-skills`,
`@topy-ai/cpo-mcp`, `@topy-ai/cpo-cli` 0.1.0.
